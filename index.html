Nasyr's Top 5 Songs List

Enter the dungeon, and see my top 5 songs list of doom! Prepare for songs by Jamie Paige, SacriStuff, Stomach Book, Flavor Foley, and Iyowa! And yes, I was kidding about the dungeon part, this songs aren't even scary or anything. And they bring you no doom! But, these songs might be a bit dark.

(The list is ordered from least to greatest)

  • #5 - Heat Abnormal by Iyowa

    Heat abnormal by Iyowa is sung by Adachi Rei and is completely in Japanese. It's about the apocalypse, and the last survivor being Adachi Rei (of whom is not a real person, it's a vocal synth) and what happened is a "black star" or a black hole is approaching. And this causes the immense heat that is talked about during the whole song. There's a lot more to dive into, but everything is hard to translate due to it being in Japanese.

    Youtube Video

  • #4 - Streetcat by Flavor Foley

    Streetcat by the grand, musical trio Flavor Foley, has a lot going on. It's sung by Nekomura Iroha (of whom is another vocal synth) and the animation and art style for the music video is wonderful, and it delves into welcoming pain to feel true satisfaction. Along with that, living in the moment, being reckless, being yourself, and risking everything across her eight lives to experience said true satisfaction.

    Youtube Video

  • #3 - Paper Dolls by Stomach Book

    Paper Dolls by Stomach Book is about grief, loss, and traumatizing dissociation after the death of a very close friend. The death of their close friend causes them to fall into grief and a frenzy, as heard in their voice while singing the song. Though I am unsure if this is based off a true story, it is very likely this happened to someone else before.

    Youtube Video

  • #2 - Astronaut by SacriStuff

    Astronaut by SacriStuff is about multiple astronauts being forced to find a new planetary system hidden somewhere deep withen the stars. It's set in the year 2097 and none of the crew wants to actually go, but then disaster strikes. Their oxygen is depleteing rapidly, and then suddenly they realize that they're now near-permanently stranded on another planet.

    Youtube Video

  • #1 - Clouddrop by Jamie Paige

    Clouddrop by Jamie Paige is a wonderful and emotional song that lasts for 6 minutes and 51 seconds. And being sung by ANRI Arcane, Kasane Teto, GUMI, and Jamie Paige herself (of whom are all vocal synths but Jamie Paige). And it's about not giving up on life, following what your dreams really are, and choosing life over death. It's based off a true story Jamie Paige themselves experienced. And I clung to this song probably because of how well the story telling is. But yet again, it's just another one of Jamie Paige's constantly good songs.

    Youtube Video
